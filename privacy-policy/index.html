Color Shoot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Color Shoot handles information when you use the application.

Color Shoot is an offline mobile game. The app does not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information in order to play the game.

Color Shoot does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or physical location for the normal operation of the game.

The app does, however, provide optional features that allow users to submit bug reports and feature suggestions. Information submitted through these features is described below.

2. Bug Reports

If you voluntarily submit a bug report through the app, the following information is sent to and stored using Google Firebase Cloud Firestore:

  • The message you enter in the report
  • The category selected for the report
  • The version of Color Shoot installed on your device
  • Your device manufacturer and device model
  • Your Android version
  • The date and time the report was submitted

Bug reports are used to identify and fix problems, improve the stability of Color Shoot, and improve the overall user experience.

3. Feature Suggestions

If you voluntarily submit a feature suggestion through the app, the following information is sent to and stored using Google Firebase Cloud Firestore:

  • The message you enter
  • The category selected for the suggestion
  • The version of Color Shoot installed on your device
  • The date and time the suggestion was submitted

Feature suggestions are used to understand user feedback and improve or develop future versions of Color Shoot.

Please do not include passwords, financial information, or other sensitive personal information in bug reports or feature suggestions.

4. Firebase and Google Cloud Firestore

Color Shoot uses Google Firebase Cloud Firestore to store bug reports and feature suggestions submitted through the app.

The information described in this Privacy Policy is transmitted to Firestore only when you choose to submit a bug report or feature suggestion.

Firebase uses encryption for data in transit, and Cloud Firestore supports encryption of stored data.

5. Advertising

The current version of Color Shoot does not display advertisements and does not use advertising services or advertising SDKs.

If advertising is introduced in a future version of Color Shoot, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant data practices before or when that version is released.

6. Information We Do Not Collect

Color Shoot does not require or intentionally collect the following information for its normal operation:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Camera data
  • Microphone or audio recordings
  • Account or login credentials
  • Payment information

The app does not require an account or sign-in.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Bug reports and feature suggestions may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to review user feedback, investigate reported problems, and improve Color Shoot.

If you have submitted a bug report or feature suggestion and would like the information associated with your submission to be deleted, you may contact the developer using the contact information below.

We will review deletion requests and delete the relevant information where reasonably possible.

8. Data Security

Color Shoot uses Google Firebase Cloud Firestore to transmit and store information submitted through the app's feedback features.

Data transmitted between the app and Firebase is protected using encryption in transit.

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through the app and limit access to the stored reports.

9. Third-Party Services

The current version of Color Shoot uses the following third-party service for the functionality described in this Privacy Policy:

  • Google Firebase Cloud Firestore — used to receive and store voluntarily submitted bug reports and feature suggestions.

Third-party services may process information in accordance with their own terms and privacy policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Color Shoot does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Users should not submit personal or sensitive information through the bug-report or feature-suggestion features.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time if the app's features or data practices change.

Any changes will be posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be updated accordingly.
